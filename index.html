Species: The King of Gir

The **Asiatic Lion** (*Panthera leo persica*) represents a crucial part of global biodiversity. Their current population is critically isolated to the **Gir Forest National Park** in Gujarat, India. This isolation makes conservation efforts paramount to prevent extinction.

Our Impact: Habitat & Community

Our mission is driven by three pillars: **Habitat Protection** (anti-poaching patrols, restoration), **Scientific Research** (population tracking, genetics), and **Community Education**. We empower local communities to coexist with these majestic animals, which is key to long-term survival.
